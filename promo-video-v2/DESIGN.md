# Webtrail Promo V2 Design

## Format

- 1080 × 1440, 3:4
- Target duration: 36–38 seconds
- Simplified Chinese voiceover
- Product-launch pacing: precise, kinetic, data-driven

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F6F8FB`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#172033`
- Muted: `#5F687A`
- Hairline: `#E3E8EF`
- Brand blue: `#1769E8`
- Deep blue: `#0F5FD7`
- Blue soft: `#EDF4FF`
- Green: `#169B68`
- Heatmap: `#F1F3F5`, `#DCE3EA`, `#AEBBC9`, `#73859A`, `#334155`

No rainbow heatmap, purple gradient, neon cyberpunk treatment, or dark full-screen scene.

## Typography

- UI and Chinese: `"Microsoft YaHei", "Segoe UI", sans-serif`
- Data: `"Consolas", monospace`
- Hero: 88–118 px, 900 weight
- Section headline: 68–82 px, 850–900 weight
- Supporting text: 28–36 px
- Data labels: 20–26 px

## Visual system

- Strong 2 px blue rules and time-axis lines
- White product panels with 24 px radius and blue-gray shadows
- Data values use tabular numerals
- Browser visit events are represented as rounded rows, nodes, and precise timestamp chips
- Light canvas gets depth from radial blue washes, ghost timestamps, grid lines, and layered product panels

## Motion language

- Click trails DRAW and CONNECT
- Hour bars GROW and WAVE across the 24-hour axis
- Day/week/month views MORPH through shared bar positions
- Calendar selection SNAPS and sends a pulse into downstream charts
- Heatmap cells ASSEMBLE diagonally, then one selected cell EXPANDS into a date detail
- Domain bars RACE and LOCK into rank
- History filters CLICK, search TYPES, rows FOLD open
- Archive particles STREAM into a local vault, then the shield LOCKS

Transitions alternate velocity push, iris reveal, hard cut, and blur-through. No repeated generic crossfades.

# Webtrail Promo V2 Storyboard

Rhythm: fast hook → precise build → morph peak → controlled hold → rapid rank → search demo → calm privacy resolve.

## Beat 1 — Click trails (0.0–3.88s)

Concept: the browser is a field of small decisions. Individual visit rows arrive from different directions and connect into one visible trail.

- BG: pale data grid, oversized ghost timestamps, restrained blue radial wash
- MG: five visit-event cards with domain, title, and time
- FG: SVG trail line, moving node, Webtrail mark
- Motion: cards STAMP in, trail DRAWS, active node TRAVELS, headline BUILDS word-by-word
- Transition: velocity-matched horizontal push

## Beat 2 — One day is not one number (3.88–8.40s)

Concept: a day opens like an instrument panel. Twenty-four bars reveal the rhythm hidden inside one selected date.

- BG: `00:00` and `23:59` ghost numerals
- MG: 24 hourly bars, selected-date calendar chip, total counter
- FG: current-hour cursor and tooltip
- Motion: axis DRAWS, bars WAVE from left to right, counter COUNTS, current hour PULSES once
- Transition: zoom-through into the time-scale selector

## Beat 3 — 24-hour day (8.40–13.90s)

Concept: the chosen date opens into 24 distinct hourly intervals.

- BG: three time lanes and oversized hour labels
- MG: 24 bars, current-hour cursor, time-range tooltip
- FG: selected date and total counter
- Motion: hour axis DRAWS; bars WAVE in; cursor SCANS and LOCKS to one interval
- Transition: hard cut into the linked dashboard

## Beat 4 — Day / week / month linked dashboard (13.90–21.70s)

Concept: the same browsing behavior is viewed through three lenses while every downstream panel stays synchronized.

- Asset: `assets/analysis.png`
- BG: cropped product screenshot in a tilted browser frame
- MG: floating `天 / 周 / 月` selector and three miniature downstream panels
- FG: active range metadata and connector lines
- Motion: toggle SNAPS; 24 bars COLLAPSE to 7, then EXPAND to 30; trend/heatmap/TOP5 cards UPDATE in sequence
- Transition: circle iris centered on the calendar

## Beat 5 — Calendar remote control (21.70–26.96s)

Concept: the calendar is not decoration; it is the remote control for every chart.

- MG: large month calendar and three downstream data panels
- FG: selected day 16, connector pulse, refreshed metric chips
- Motion: date 16 CLICKS, pulse RADIATES, three panels UPDATE left-to-right
- Transition: directional blur to the data map

## Beat 6 — Heatmap and domain ranking (26.96–32.95s)

Concept: long-term behavior becomes a tactile map, then resolves into a clear attention hierarchy.

- MG: 7 × 18 neutral heatmap above five domain rows
- FG: selected heat cell detail and `TOP 5` label
- Motion: cells ASSEMBLE diagonally; bars RACE; counts TICK; final ranks LOCK
- Transition: vertical push into history

## Beat 7 — BetterHistory precision (32.95–36.25s)

Concept: a lost page is recovered through a sequence of deliberate filters.

- Asset: `assets/history.png`
- BG: full history screenshot in a deep browser frame
- MG: search field, date/domain/type filter chips, highlighted result rows
- FG: cursor and result count
- Motion: query TYPES, filters CLICK, irrelevant rows DIM, matching rows FOLD open
- Transition: white flash-through

## Beat 8 — Local archive / CTA (36.25–39.94s)

Concept: all trails converge safely into a local vault. Energy resolves into trust.

- Assets: `assets/webtrail-icon.png`, `assets/analysis.png`, `assets/history.png`
- BG: two floating product panels and a quiet radial wash
- MG: archive vault, shield, stream of visit nodes
- FG: Webtrail mark, privacy promise, final line
- Motion: nodes STREAM, vault CLOSES, shield LOCKS, final statement SETTLES
- Resolve: 1.5-second clean hold
